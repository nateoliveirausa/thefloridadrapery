Frequently asked questions

Quick answers before your free estimate.

Is the in-home estimate really free?

Yes. We visit your home, measure your windows, show fabric samples and give you a written quote. There is no cost and no obligation.

Which areas of Florida do you serve?

We serve homeowners across Florida. Send us your address and ZIP code and we will confirm your visit.

How much does custom drapery cost?

Price depends on window size, style, fabric and hardware. That is why we measure first. You receive one clear written price for fabric, making and installation before any work begins. Have another quote? Show it to us.

What styles do you offer?

Sheer curtains, cornice drapery, wall-to-wall ripple fold and hotel-style layered drapes. Not sure which fits your room? Choose "Not sure yet" and we will help you decide during the visit.

Do you measure and install?

Yes. Our team measures every window on site and installs the finished drapery, so the person who measures is the person who installs.

How long does it take?

Timing depends on the project size and fabric. We give you a schedule in writing with your quote so you know what to expect.

Can I get light control and privacy together?

Yes. Layered drapes pair a sheer for daytime privacy and soft light with a heavier drape for full light control at night.

Do you work with designers, builders or hotels?

Yes. We work with interior designers, builders, property managers and hospitality projects. Call or text (813) 500-0143 to talk about your project.

How do I get started?

Fill out the form at the top of this page, or call, text or WhatsApp (813) 500-0143. We will contact you to schedule your visit.
