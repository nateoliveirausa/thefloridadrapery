Questions, answered

Will sheers give me privacy at night?

Sheers are a daytime privacy solution. With the lights on at night, people can see shapes inside. If you need night privacy or darkness, we pair your sheers with a lined drape or shade and quote both options.

Can sheers cover very wide sliding doors?

Yes. Wide sliders and tall ceilings are exactly where custom work matters most. We measure the full wall and build the curtain to span it cleanly.

How much does it cost?

Every window is different, so we price after measuring: your exact width, height and fabric. The in-home estimate is free and the quote is written, with no surprise charges on install day. Our promise is simple: the best service at the best price. If you have another quote, show it to us.
